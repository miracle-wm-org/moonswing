## What it is

Moonswing draws a whole desktop session out of one Flutter process: `wlr-layer-shell`
panels, a wallpaper and desktop-icon surface, full-screen overlays, popups, an on-screen
display and a lock screen. It is also the session's notification daemon, its
StatusNotifierItem tray host, its `xdg-desktop-portal` ScreenCast backend, its polkit
authentication agent and its session locker.

It exists to:

1. Stress test Flutter
2. Explore the bounds of building new desktops for Wayland
3. Experiment with new Wayland protocols, portals, and everything else
4. Have fun and build something fun

## Your data

Moonswing runs entirely on your own computer, and the project runs no server. You can
optionally sign in to one or more Google accounts under Settings › Accounts, which shows
your calendar events in the shell's Calendar tab and can put today's meetings on the todo board. That
access is read-only, and your calendar goes straight from Google to your machine. A GitHub
account can be linked there too, for the notification inbox on the bar.
